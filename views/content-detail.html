Breaking Bad

TV Show · 2008 · TV-MA · crime · English

A chemistry teacher turned drug kingpin navigates a dangerous criminal world.

Season 1, Episode 1 — Pilot

Walter White, a struggling high school chemistry teacher, is diagnosed with terminal cancer. To secure his family's future, he partners with a former student to cook and sell methamphetamine, taking his first dangerous steps into a hidden criminal world.

Season 1, Episode 2 — Cat’s in the Bag…

Walt and Jesse are left scrambling to clean up the aftermath of their first cook. As the pressure mounts and their cover story grows thin, both men discover how far they are willing to go to protect themselves and each other.
